# Experiment 3 - E-Commerce App UI: Resolving Stakeholder Conflict (Figma)
**CO1** | Tool: Figma

## Scenario
Stakeholders disagree on the mobile UI. The prototype presents the options side by side so feedback can be collected quickly.

## Stakeholder views
| Stakeholder | Preference | Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Large banners, promotions first | Drive campaign visibility |
| Product / UX | Clean grid, search-first | Fast product discovery |
| Sales | One-tap Add to Cart on cards | Higher conversion |
| Management | Minimal checkout steps | Reduce cart abandonment |

## Prototype plan
Create **two variants** of the Home and Product screens:
- **Variant A - Promotion-led**: hero banner, category carousel, deals.
- **Variant B - Discovery-led**: sticky search, category chips, 2-column product grid.

Shared screens: Product Detail, Cart, 3-step Checkout, Order Success.

## Feedback process
1. Share the prototype link with *Can comment* access.
2. Walk stakeholders through both flows (15 min each).
3. Collect comments in Figma and record them in `feedback-log.md`.
4. Decide using the criteria in the log, produce the merged design (Variant C) and get sign-off.
